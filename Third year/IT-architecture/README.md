# IT Architecture

This folder contains seminar assignments, reports and practical exercises completed during the **IT Architecture** course at Dalarna University.

The course focused on analyzing organizations and information systems from multiple perspectives. Through a fictional company case, various enterprise architecture frameworks, modeling techniques and knowledge representation methods were applied to describe business goals, stakeholders, processes, information flows and organizational structures.

## Seminar 1 – Enterprise Description

The first seminar introduced a fictional company that served as the foundation for the remainder of the course.

### Activities

- Defining business goals and objectives
- Identifying products and services
- Identifying stakeholders
- Describing organizational structures
- Mapping business processes
- Introducing enterprise architecture perspectives

The objective was to establish a business context that could later be analyzed using architectural frameworks and modeling techniques.

---

## Seminar 2 – Business Architecture & Process Modeling

The company was analyzed using several business and enterprise architecture frameworks.

### Methods Used

- **Balanced Scorecard (BSC)**
- **Business Model Canvas (BMC)**
- **Business Process Modeling Notation (BPMN)**

### Focus Areas

- Strategic objectives
- Stakeholder perspectives
- Business value creation
- Process analysis
- Organizational concerns

The seminar explored how different stakeholders view the same organization through different architectural perspectives.

---

## Seminar 3 – Conceptual & Enterprise Modeling

This seminar focused on describing the organization through conceptual and enterprise models.

### Models Created

- **Objectives Model (OM)**
- **Data Flow Diagram (DFD)**
- **Entity Relationship Model (ER)**
- **Enterprise Model (EM)**

### Topics Covered

- Business goals
- Problems and opportunities
- Information flows
- Data structures
- Relationships between models

The purpose was to understand how goals, processes, information and data interact within an enterprise.

---

## Seminar 4 – Ontology Engineering

The final seminar focused on knowledge management and ontology modeling using **Protégé** and **OWL (Web Ontology Language)**.

### Activities

- Creating classes and subclasses
- Defining object properties
- Defining data properties
- Creating individuals (instances)
- Validating the ontology using reasoning

The goal was to model organizational knowledge and identify logical inconsistencies within the knowledge base.
